The long wait

How long undertrials had been inside on 31 December 2024. Most stays are short, but the tail is not.

1,11,832undertrials (30.1%)

1,11,832 undertrials (30%) had been held for more than a year, and 9,028 for more than five. In all of 2024, only 627 undertrials were released under Section 479 BNSS (earlier s. 436A CrPC), which lets an undertrial go after serving part of the maximum sentence.

Room to hold them

Occupancy is falling, from 131.4% in 2022 to 112.7% in 2024. Capacity grew while the population shrank.

The long view

Undertrials made up about 65% of prisoners through the 2000s, and the share climbed after 2013. It peaked at 77% in 2021 and has fallen every year since.
